Privacy policy

Applies to the Dance Cabinet app. Last updated 21 November 2026.

What data the app reads

With the read-only Google permission youtube.readonly, the app reads the signed-in owner's list of liked videos: each video's ID, title, channel name and category.

How the data is used

Only to find songs that can be added to the owner's own home dance machine. The app does not read anything else from the Google account.

Where it is kept

The list and the sign-in token stay on the owner's own computers (a personal computer and the dance machine). Nothing is uploaded to any other server, and there are no ads, analytics or tracking.

Sharing

The data is never sold, shared or given to anyone. The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

The owner can withdraw the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and can delete the stored list and token from their own computers.

Contact

This is a personal, single-user app. Questions can be raised through this site's GitHub issues.
