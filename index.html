Dance Cabinet

Dance Cabinet is a private tool for one home dance-game machine. It reads its owner's list of liked music on YouTube, so new favourite songs can be added to the machine's song list.

Who it is for

Only the owner of that machine. It is not offered to the public, has no sign-up, and no one else's account is used.

What it does with Google

It asks for one read-only permission, View your YouTube account, and uses it only to list the owner's liked videos. It never posts, likes, comments, uploads or changes anything.
